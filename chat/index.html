FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which channels does Cekat Chat work with?

WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Messenger, Telegram, web chat and email — in one inbox.

Can the AI hand a chat over to my team?

Yes. When a question needs a person, it hands the chat to your team.

Does the AI only answer from my own data?

Yes. It answers from the knowledge you give it, and hands off instead of guessing.

Can I choose when the AI works?

Yes. Set schedules, like AI at night and your team by day, and choose the AI’s share of chats.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, there’s a 14-day free trial, and setup takes about 10 minutes.
