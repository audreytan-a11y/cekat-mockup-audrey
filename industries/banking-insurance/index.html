FAQ

Questions financial teams
often ask.

How is customer data kept secure?

Chats and contact details stay inside your Cekat workspace, and you control which team members can see them. Cekat is ISO/IEC 27001 certified and compliant with Indonesia’s UU PDP data protection law. Our team can walk you through how data is handled for your business.

Can we route chats to different teams?

Yes. Connect your WhatsApp numbers and other channels to one inbox, and route each chat to the right team, like claims, cards or loans.

How long does setup take?

It depends on how many channels you connect and how much product and policy information you want the AI to learn. Our team guides you through connecting WhatsApp and setting up your AI agent, step by step.

Will the AI give financial advice?

No. You decide what the AI answers. It sticks to the product information you give it, and hands advice, account-specific and sensitive requests to your team.
