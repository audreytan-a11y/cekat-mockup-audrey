FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which ad platforms can I connect?

Meta Ads Manager, TikTok and Google. Sync them and every ad shows up in one dashboard.

Do I need a developer to set it up?

No. Each source connects in 1–2 steps, with no manual data set-up and no IT team.

How does Cekat stop ad sales being counted as organic?

When a buyer shares a network with someone who saw your ad, Cekat detects it and ties the purchase back to the ad.

What does the Meta Conversions API do here?

It sends stages like Meetings booked and Executed meeting back to Meta, so it targets people who actually buy.

Can I message the leads I see?

Yes. Export the list, or broadcast in one click with a message that fits what each lead did.
