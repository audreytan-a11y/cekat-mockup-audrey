FAQ

Questions clinics
often ask.

How is patient data kept secure?

Chats and contact details stay inside your Cekat workspace, and you control which team members can see them. Cekat is ISO/IEC 27001 certified and compliant with Indonesia’s UU PDP data protection law. Our team can walk you through how data is handled for your clinic.

Can we use it across multiple branches?

Yes. Connect each branch’s WhatsApp number and other channels to one inbox, route chats to the right branch team, and keep every branch’s conversations clearly separated.

How long does setup take?

It depends on how many channels you connect and how much clinic information you want the AI to learn. Our team guides you through connecting WhatsApp and setting up your AI agent, step by step.

Will the AI give medical advice?

You decide what the AI answers. It sticks to the information you give it, like hours, prices and doctors, and hands sensitive or medical questions over to your staff.
